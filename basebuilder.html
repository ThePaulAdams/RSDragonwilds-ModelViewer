Base

Connect the game folder to see your base and anchor.

Build your base here, see it in your game

Needs the Custom Builds mod (in the RuneScape: Dragonwilds Toolkit) and Chrome or Edge.

  1. In the game, stand where you want to build (or aim at the spot) and type cb anchor in the UE4SS console (F10). That saves the spot and your building pieces around it.
  2. Here, click Connect game folder and pick RSDragonwilds\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\CustomBuilds. Allow editing when the browser asks.
  3. Your anchor shows as a gold marker and your base as grey shapes. Pick models on the left and click to place them.
  4. Every change is saved into the mod's placed.txt and appears in the game within a couple of seconds. Things you place, move or delete in the game show up here too.

Controls

W move   E rotate   R scale   G snap on/off   F focus selection
Delete delete   Ctrl+D duplicate   Ctrl+Z undo   Esc stop placing / deselect

No game folder? Use Export to download placed.txt and copy it into that folder yourself (with the game closed).
