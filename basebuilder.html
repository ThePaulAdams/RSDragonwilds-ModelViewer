📋 Installation Steps

  1. Prerequisite — Install UE4SS Experimental (UE 5.6):
    RuneScape: Dragonwilds runs on Unreal Engine 5.6. Download the experimental UE4SS release compiled for UE 5.6.
    Extract the UE4SS files directly into your game's binary folder:
    <SteamLibrary>\steamapps\common\RSDragonwilds\RSDragonwilds\Binaries\Win64\
    (You will see ue4ss.dll, proxy DLL e.g. dwmapi.dll, and a ue4ss/ directory).
  2. Download the Ashenfallen Modpack:
    Download the latest release zip from CurseForge Ashenfallen Modpack.
  3. Extract into your Mods folder:
    Unzip the modpack contents directly into:
    <SteamLibrary>\steamapps\common\RSDragonwilds\RSDragonwilds\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\
    Your folder structure will look like this:
    RSDragonwilds\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\ ├── CustomBuilds\ <-- Live piece & interactive quest engine │ ├── scripts\main.lua │ ├── placed.txt │ └── quests.json ├── OSRSMinimap\ <-- Old School RuneScape HUD minimap ├── PauseGuard\ <-- Anti-freeze & pause safeguard ├── QuickStack\ <-- Instant 1-click chest sorting ├── AutoRun\ <-- Auto-sprint / run toggle └── mods.txt <-- Enables the mods
  4. Launch RuneScape: Dragonwilds:
    Start the game via Steam. UE4SS loads your mods automatically! Press F10 in-game if you want to open the developer console.
✨ What's Included in the Modpack?
• CustomBuilds: Reads web layouts & custom quests, spawning models and companion NPCs live in your world.
• PauseGuard: Prevents game desyncs and freezing while paused or saving.
• OSRSMinimap: Classic Old School RuneScape minimap overlay with square/round toggle and player radar.
• QuickStack: Automatically deposits matching items into nearby chests.
Method 1 ⚡ Live In-Game 2-Way Sync (Recommended)

Connect your browser directly to your game folder using the modern File System Access API (Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera).

  1. Click the green Connect game folder button in the top toolbar.
  2. In the folder dialog, select your main RSDragonwilds game folder (Steam Library > right-click RuneScape: Dragonwilds > Manage > Browse local files). The builder automatically locates your CustomBuilds mod directory!
  3. Auto-Anchor to Player: Stand in-game where you want your building or camp. The web builder auto-anchors to your live player coordinates! Click 👤 Player (or press P) to fly the camera right to your character.
  4. Place & Watch: Browse over 5,100+ authentic models on the left, click to place them, and adjust with gizmos. Every movement or piece placed saves to placed.txt and materializes live in your game immediately!
Method 2 💾 Manual Export & Import (Offline / Any Browser)

Don't want to connect your folder live or using Firefox / Safari? You can still build offline!

  • Build and design your base freely in this 3D canvas.
  • Click Export in the top toolbar to download your placed.txt file.
  • Copy placed.txt into RSDragonwilds\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\CustomBuilds\.
  • When you launch the game or enter the world, all pieces will spawn around your anchor position.
  • Click Import anytime to re-load an existing placed.txt back into the web builder.
Method 3 🔍 Mod-Free Save Game Inspector

Want to see your vanilla game base in 3D without installing any mods?

  • Click Open save in the top toolbar.
  • Select Wilds.sav from %LOCALAPPDATA%\RSDragonwilds\Saved\SaveGames\.
  • Your full vanilla building pieces load in full 3D surrounded by authentic terrain. Completely read-only and safe!
Storyboarding 📜 Creating Custom Interactive Quests & Companions

Turn your base into a living RuneScape settlement! Spawn iconic NPCs, write dialogue trees, and reward players with real items and XP.

  1. Place Companion NPCs: Search for characters in the left library (e.g. Doric, Cook, Wise Old Man, Father Aereck, guards, shopkeepers) and place them in your base.
  2. Open Storyboard Editor: Select the placed NPC and click 📜 Storyboard for this NPC in the right sidebar (or click 📜 Quests in the top toolbar).
  3. Configure Dialogue & Choices: Write NPC lines, add player response options (e.g. "I can help with that!"), and configure branching quest paths.
  4. Set Objectives & 3D Waypoints: Require players to talk to NPCs, collect specific items (ores, logs, runes, food), or visit coordinates. Click 📍 Pick in 3D to set the exact waypoint location in the 3D scene!
  5. Assign In-Game Rewards: Configure gold, skill XP (Smithing, Cooking, Combat), or item packs awarded to the player upon quest completion.
  6. Live In-Game Interaction: NPCs display animated 3D ! exclamation markers overhead in-game. Walk up to them and press E to initiate dialogue and start the quest!
