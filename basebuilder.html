Build your base here, see it in your game

Works with or without mods. Live in-game sync needs the Custom Builds mod (in the RuneScape: Dragonwilds Toolkit) and Chrome or Edge.

Load your game save (No mods needed): Click Open save and pick Wilds.sav from %LOCALAPPDATA%\RSDragonwilds\Saved\SaveGames. Your full base loads instantly in 3D right where you built it, surrounded by the real game terrain and world map. The save is only read, never modified.

To build and sync with your game:

  1. Click Connect game folder and pick your main RSDragonwilds game folder (Steam: right-click game > Manage > Browse local files). The builder automatically locates your mod directory or saves placed.txt right there.
  2. In the game, stand where you want to build and the builder will automatically anchor to your live player position (or type cb anchor in console F10).
  3. Pick models from the library (or click any world object to eyedropper it) and place them down. Every change saves into placed.txt and updates live in your game!
